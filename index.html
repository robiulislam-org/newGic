🔒 Privacy Policy

Safeguarding your personal data and privacy is our highest commitment.

📋 Information We Collect

Global Islamic Care may collect the following information when you register or communicate with us:

  • Parent & student full name and contact number (WhatsApp/Phone)
  • Country and city of residence (for timezone scheduling)
  • Learning goals and selected Quran program
  • Student age and family members for group sessions
  • Transaction confirmation details for tuition processing

🎯 How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used solely for the following educational purposes:

  • To schedule and coordinate 1-on-1 live mentoring sessions
  • To provide payment confirmations and electronic receipts
  • To deliver academic progress updates and course notices
  • To enhance pedagogical quality and student support
  • To respond to inquiries and provide parent support

🛡️ Data Security & Protection

We are committed to maintaining data privacy and security:

  • Your personal data is NEVER sold, leased, or shared with third parties
  • Access is restricted solely to authorized mentors and academic coordinators
  • Direct communications are encrypted through secure channels
  • Financial data is handled securely and never stored on public servers

📞 Communication Channels

We communicate with parents regarding:

  • Session schedules and academic updates via WhatsApp
  • Monthly tuition reminders at the beginning of each cycle
  • Important academy announcements when necessary
You may request communication preferences at any time. We fully respect your family's choices.
